## Game

### Dependecies
`pygame`

### Commands:
`python main.py`

<br>

### Controls:
`W` or `↑` – Move upwards <br>
`A` or `←` – Move to the left <br>
`S` or `↓` – Move downwards <br>
`D` or `→` – Move to the right<br>
